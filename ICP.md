# ICP: small and mid-size only

| Segment | Who | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| A | Agencies of 1 to 30 people (SEO, content, digital) with no GEO offer yet | Angle: add an AI-readiness audit to what you sell |
| A2 | Small agencies and freelancers that already sell GEO | Angle: tool/API partnership. Never call anyone a "partner" in copy before it exists |
| B | B2B SaaS with 5 to 100 employees (seed to Series A) that does content marketing, including developer tools | Angle: crawler access and schema gaps on their own site |
| C | Boutique professional-services firms with 5 to 50 people (consulting, accounting, legal) | Angle: be readable by AI crawlers before buyers ask |

Excluded: 200+ employees, enterprise agency networks, public companies, Fortune lists, competitors that sell AI-readiness scanning (they go to a separate list).
Markets: US, UK, Canada, Australia, EU (English-language sites). Turkey is excluded for now.

The prospect agent (local, `~/dev/citehound-outbound`) applies this in `config/icp.yaml`. Its output to the desk is aggregate counts and company-level cards only.
